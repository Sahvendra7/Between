MEDIATION MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR SOFTWARE TESTING. NOT A REAL AGREEMENT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.

Present: Jess Moreau ("Mom") and Alex Moreau ("Dad"), with mediator.
The parents have one child.

Item 1. Schedule
Dad has the child every Wednesday overnight and every other weekend.

Item 2. Childcare
Daycare is split 50/50. Dad pays the daycare directly each month and Mom reimburses him half within 10 days of his request.

Item 3. Doctor and dentist
Medical and dental bills not covered by insurance: Dad pays 70%, Mom pays 30%.

=== Page 2 ===
Item 4. Big expenses
Anything over 300 dollars has to be discussed and agreed in writing before it is paid, whatever it is for.

Item 5. Summer camp
Dad covers summer camp up to $800 in total each summer. Any amount above $800 is split 50/50.

Item 6. School supplies
Whoever buys school supplies pays for them. They are not shared.
