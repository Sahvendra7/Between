PARENTING PLAN
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR SOFTWARE TESTING. NOT A REAL AGREEMENT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.

Parties: Maria Rivera ("the Mother") and David Okafor ("the Father").
Child: one minor child ("the Child").

Section 1 — Purpose
1.1 This plan sets out how the parents share parenting time and the costs of raising the Child.

Section 2 — Parenting Time
2.1 The Child lives with each parent on alternate weeks, changing over on Sundays at 6:00 p.m.

Section 6 — Shared Child Expenses
6.1 Medical. Unreimbursed medical, dental, orthodontic and vision expenses for the Child shall be paid 60% by the Father and 40% by the Mother.
6.2 Extracurricular activities. The parents shall share the cost of extracurricular activities equally, 50% each. Neither parent shall enrol the Child in an activity costing more than $200 without the prior written consent of the other parent.
6.3 Annual limit. Neither parent shall be required to contribute more than $1,500 per calendar year toward extracurricular activities.
6.4 Education. Tutoring, school trips and school fees shall be shared equally, 50% each.

Section 7 — Reimbursement Procedure
7.1 A parent seeking reimbursement shall send the other parent a request with a copy of the receipt within 30 days of paying the expense.
7.2 The other parent shall pay their share within 14 days of receiving a complete request.
7.3 Clothing, gifts and everyday food are not shared expenses; each parent pays these costs in their own household.

Section 8 — General
8.1 Nothing in this plan prevents the parents from agreeing in writing to share any other expense.
